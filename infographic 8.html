Technology Timeline

The First Computers

The ENIAC, completed in 1945, was the first general-purpose electronic computer. Weighing 30 tons and occupying 1,800 square feet, it could perform 5,000 additions per second.

1945

Transistor Invention

Bell Labs invented the transistor in 1947, replacing vacuum tubes. This breakthrough made computers smaller, faster, and more reliable, paving the way for modern computing.

1947

Integrated Circuits

Jack Kilby and Robert Noyce independently invented the integrated circuit in 1958-1959. This allowed multiple transistors to be placed on a single silicon chip.

1958

Personal Computers

The Altair 8800, released in 1975, was the first commercially successful personal computer. This sparked the PC revolution, leading to Apple and IBM PCs.

1975

World Wide Web

Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. This created the foundation for the modern internet as we know it today.

1989

Smartphone Revolution

Apple introduced the iPhone in 2007, revolutionizing mobile computing. Touchscreens, app ecosystems, and mobile internet became mainstream.

2007
