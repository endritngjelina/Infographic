Rising Temperatures

+1.2°C

Global Temperature Increase Since 1880

The Earth's average temperature has risen by 1.2°C since the late 19th century. The last decade was the warmest on record, with each year consistently ranking among the hottest ever measured.

This warming is primarily driven by increased carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gas emissions from human activities.

Melting Ice Caps

-13%

Arctic Sea Ice Decline Per Decade

Arctic sea ice is declining at a rate of 13% per decade. The ice acts as Earth's reflector, bouncing sunlight back to space. As it melts, more dark ocean is exposed, absorbing heat and accelerating warming.

Greenland and Antarctica are losing ice mass, contributing to rising sea levels that threaten coastal communities worldwide.

Solutions in Action

We have the technology and knowledge to address climate change. Here's how we can make a difference:

🌱

Renewable Energy

Solar and wind power costs have dropped by 90% in the last decade. Transitioning to clean energy is now economically viable and environmentally essential.

🌳

Reforestation

Forests absorb CO₂ from the atmosphere. Protecting existing forests and planting new ones can significantly impact carbon reduction efforts.

⚡

Energy Efficiency

Improving energy efficiency in buildings, transportation, and industry can reduce emissions while saving money and resources.

🔬

Innovation

Carbon capture technology, green hydrogen, and sustainable materials are emerging solutions that will help us achieve net-zero emissions.
